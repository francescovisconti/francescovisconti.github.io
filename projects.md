## MEDem – Monitoring Electoral Democracy

**MEDem** is a European research infrastructure for the study of electoral
democracy in Europe. In 2026 it was included in the **ESFRI Roadmap**, the list of research
infrastructures of strategic importance for the European Research Area.

Since 2025 I have been part of the **Luiss team** of MEDem, working on
**[ATLAS](https://atlas-medem.iac.rm.cnr.it/)** within the Service Center run
by Luiss and CNR-IAC. ATLAS is a concept-aware platform for data harmonisation:
a knowledge graph links theoretical concepts, their dimensions and indicators to
the actual survey questions that measure them, so that comparable variables can
be found and combined across datasets. My work focuses on the
**Data Annotation Kit (DAK)**, the tool researchers use to annotate their
datasets, and on its R parser.

- Presentation of ATLAS to the SISP Standing Group
  [POPE](https://standinggroups.sisp.it/pope/), SISP Annual Conference, Trento, September 2026.
- MEDem Conference, GESIS, Cologne, September 2025; MEDem workshops in Vienna
  (January 2025 and May 2026, on DAK user feedback).

## The 2026 constitutional referendum

A two-wave online panel fielded by Demetra for Luiss and the University of
Southern Denmark around the March 2026 constitutional referendum on the
judiciary (Wave I one month before the vote, n = 2,520; Wave II right after it,
n = 2,192). I use its open-ended items to study how Yes voters, No voters and
abstainers justify their choice.

- [Blog post: *Yeasayers, naysayers and non-voters*](/2026-09-24-yeasayers-naysayers-and-non-voters/)
- Paper presented at the SISP Annual Conference, Trento, September 2026.
