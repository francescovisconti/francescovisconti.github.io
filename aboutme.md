I am **Assistant Professor of Political Science (RTT)** at the
[Department of Political Science](https://scienzepolitiche.luiss.it/) of
[Luiss Guido Carli](https://www.luiss.it), where I teach public opinion,
elections, and social research methods.

I came to political science through a simple question: when do citizens'
preferences actually reach the decisions that are made in their name? That
question has taken me from legislative agendas in Italy to public support for
European solidarity, from the political consequences of free movement to the
politicisation of climate change.

Methodologically, I work mostly with comparative survey data — including surveys
I have helped design and field — survey experiments, and quantitative text
analysis. I use R and I care about replicable research.

Before joining Luiss, I was a researcher at the Italian National Research
Council, the University of Milan, and the University of Leicester. I hold a PhD
in Comparative and European Politics from the
[University of Siena](https://www.unisi.it) (2017).
