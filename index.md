I am **Assistant Professor (RTT) of Political Science** at the
[Department of Political Science](https://scienzepolitiche.luiss.it/) of
[Luiss Guido Carli](https://www.luiss.it) in Rome and a member of [CISE](https://cise.luiss.it/), Centro Italiano Studi Elettorali.

My research asks how European citizens respond to open borders and to a climate
crisis no country can face alone — and what those responses mean for parties,
elections, and social policy. I work with comparative survey data, survey
experiments, and quantitative text analysis.

### Research at a glance

**European solidarity** — Who is willing to share risks across member states,
and how that willingness reshapes political competition in the EU.

**Free movement** — How transnational experience changes political attitudes and
behaviour, and how concern about emigration enters the ballot box.

**Climate politics** — Public support for the energy transition and eco-social
policies, and how climate became a politicised issue in Italy.
