I am **Assistant Professor (RTT) of Political Science** at the
[Department of Political Science](https://scienzepolitiche.luiss.it/) of
[Luiss Guido Carli](https://www.luiss.it) in Rome and a member of [CISE](https://cise.luiss.it/), the _Centro Italiano Studi Elettorali_.

My research asks how European citizens respond to open borders and to the climate
crisis — and what those responses mean for parties,
elections, and social policy. I work mostly with comparative survey data, survey
experiments, and quantitative text analysis.

### Research at a glance

**European solidarity** — Who is willing to share risks across member states,
and how that willingness reshapes political competition in the EU.

**Free movement** — How transnational experience changes political attitudes and
behaviour, and how concern about emigration enters the ballot box.

**Climate politics** — Public support for the energy transition and eco-social
policies, and how climate became a politicised issue in Italy.

**Research infrastructures** — As part of the Luiss team of
[MEDem](/projects#medem), the growing European infrastructure on electoral democracy now
on the ESFRI Roadmap, I help develop [ATLAS](https://atlas-medem.iac.rm.cnr.it/atlas/home), an innovative platform for data linkage and harmonization.
